Let’s meet
the real me

With a decade of experience in the film industry, I am a dedicated cinematographer passionate about capturing the essence of stories through the lens. Based in Kolkata and Mumbai, I actively seek collaborations with creative individuals to bring innovative and impactful visuals to life. My commitment to pushing creative boundaries and embracing challenges has allowed me to evolve as an artist and contribute to the cinematic landscape.

About Rhea

How it all started

A distinguished cinematographer with a prolific career spanning over a decade, Rhea Kapoor has made significant contributions to the Indian film industry. She holds a degree in Cinematography from a leading film institute and is a member of the Indian Society of Cinematographers (ISC) and other cinematographers’ collectives.

With a portfolio spanning over 30 short films, TV commercials, documentaries, corporate films and feature films, her work encompasses a diverse range of genres and styles. Her projects have earned multiple awards at national and international film festivals, and she is committed to advancing gender inclusion in the technical aspects of filmmaking.
