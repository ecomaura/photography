Let’s meet the real me

“With a decade of experience in the film industry, I am a dedicated cinematographer passionate about capturing the essence of stories through the lens. Based in Kolkata and Mumbai, I actively seek collaborations with creative individuals to bring innovative and impactful visuals to life. Since my career began in 2017, I have had the privilege of working on diverse projects across various genres. My commitment to pushing creative boundaries and embracing challenges has allowed me to evolve as an artist and contribute to the cinematic landscape.”

About Rhea

How it all started

A distinguished cinematographer with a prolific career spanning over a decade, Rhea Kapoor has made significant contributions to the Indian film industry. She holds a degree in Cinematography from a leading film and television institute and is a member of the Indian Society of Cinematographers (ISC) and other cinematographers’ collectives. With a portfolio spanning over 30 short films, TV commercials, documentaries, corporate films, and feature films, her work encompasses a diverse range of genres and styles.

Her projects have received both critical and commercial acclaim, earning multiple awards at national and international film festivals. She is committed to advancing gender inclusion in the technical aspects of filmmaking, inspiring the next generation of women professionals in the industry.

Asian Film Academy

Was a fellow of AFA 2015. Asian Film Academy (AFA) is an educational program hosted by Busan International Film Festival, Dongseo University and Busan Film Commission to foster young Asian talents and build their networks throughout Asia. The fellows participate in various programs, including short film production, workshops, and master classes, and the two short films they complete are officially presented at the Busan International Film Festival.

Award

Pierre Angénieux ExcelLens in Cinematography

Special Encouragement Award, Cannes 2019. The Pierre Angénieux ExcelLens in Cinematography ceremony at the Festival de Cannes recognised the work of young and upcoming cinematographer Rhea Kapoor, making her the first Indian — and first Indian woman — to be recognised in this category.

Experience

Looking China Youth Film Project

Participated as part of the Looking China 2015 Youth Film Project, representing India. Travelled to China and directed, shot, edited and produced a short documentary in an 18 day time frame; the film received the 2nd Runner up award. Was part of the 10 member Indian team among 100 filmmakers from all over the globe.

Experience

Berlinale Talents 2023

Berlinale Talents is the talent summit and networking programme of the Berlin International Film Festival. An annual platform for 200 outstanding creatives from the fields of film and drama series, it is one of the biggest talent summits in the world, supporting participants in positioning themselves in the filmmaking industry.

Honoured at Sera Bangali Awards 2024

Rhea Kapoor, a skilled cinematographer, was honoured in 2024, recognizing her significant contributions to the fields of arts and culture. Her work has consistently impressed both audiences and critics, earning her accolades at numerous national and international film festivals.
